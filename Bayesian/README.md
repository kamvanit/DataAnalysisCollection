The notebook "Jose_toy_model_perfect_measurements_Omk.ipynb" presented a statistical analysis of the simulated data done in Python. Where the statistical method was 'Bayesian inference' and the data represented GW(gravitational wave) detections.

With the statistical inference, it showed what kind of medium GW has traveled through. 
In this case GW travel through the universe that is expanding, at a rate predicted by Hubble, 
i.e. it agrees with the common belief that the universe it flat, and dark matter does not move in a crazy way. 

With a large number of simulated data point, we can study GW that traveled from various sources and distances from earth. One can then see a pattern of their evolution and so we can study the cosmology from that.
